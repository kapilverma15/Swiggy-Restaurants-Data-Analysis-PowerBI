# 🍔 Swiggy Restaurants Data Analysis Dashboard

An interactive Power BI dashboard designed to analyze restaurant density, pricing trends, customer ratings, and cuisine preferences across major Indian cities and localities.

## 🎯 Business Problem & Objective
The goal of this project is to analyze food delivery market dynamics to help restaurant owners and aggregators understand:
* Overall market benchmarks for pricing, ratings, and delivery turnaround times.
* Customer demand and distribution across top food categories (cuisines).
* Restaurant concentration across tier-1 cities and local neighborhoods.
* High-end dining hubs vs. value-oriented locations based on average pricing.

---

## 📈 Key Metrics & Insights
* **Market Scale:** Analyzed **8,680+ restaurants** across top metro hubs.
* **Core Benchmarks:**
  * **Average Rating:** 3.66 / 5.0
  * **Average Price:** ₹348.44
  * **Average Delivery Time:** 53.97 minutes
* **Cuisine Demand (Top 5):** 
  * **Indian** dominates market share at **28.19%** (389 listings), followed by **Chinese (20.07%)**, **North Indian (17.83%)**, **Fast Food (17.39%)**, and **South Indian (16.52%)**.
* **High-Density Hubs:** Rohini (257) and Chembur (208) host the largest restaurant volumes.
* **Premium Dining Clusters:** Areas like Vile Parle (~1.5K avg price) and Pacific Mall Subhash Nagar lead in premium pricing.
* **Metro Concentration:** Maximum restaurant density observed across Delhi, Mumbai, Pune, Ahmedabad, and Hyderabad.

---

## 🛠️ Tech Stack & Skills
* **BI Tool:** Microsoft Power BI Desktop
* **Data Transformation:** Power Query (ETL, Null handling, data type casting, text cleaning)
* **Formulas & Modeling:** DAX (Custom measures for `Avg Rating`, `Avg Price`, `Avg Delivery Time`, `Total Restaurants`)
* **Visualizations:** Donut Chart (Top 5 Cuisines via Top N filter), Clustered Bar Charts, Geographic Map, Multi-Row KPI Cards, Dynamic Slicers (City, Area)
